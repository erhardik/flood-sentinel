# flood-sentinel
An open-source initiative to explore AI + IoT + GIS based hyperlocal flood early warning systems for buildings, basements, shops and societies. We welcome researchers, students, engineers and domain experts to collaborate. This project is experimental and research-driven, not a production safety system.

We don't claim to have the solution. We are building a community to find one. Every flood teaches us something. Every contributor brings a piece of the answer. Whether you are a student, an experienced engineer, a researcher, or simply someone with an idea, you are welcome to contribute. Even if this approach ultimately fails, the lessons learned may lead to a better, more affordable innovation.
