# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Flood Sentinel is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for all participants. We are dedicated to creating a community where everyone feels safe, valued, and able to contribute their best work, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, political belief, race, religion, sex, sexual identity and orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that creates a positive environment include:

- **Respect and Inclusivity**: Treating all community members with respect and dignity
- **Active Listening**: Genuinely hearing and considering others' perspectives
- **Constructive Feedback**: Providing helpful, thoughtful critique focused on improvement
- **Collaboration**: Working together toward shared goals and celebrating others' contributions
- **Transparency**: Being honest about limitations, uncertainties, and failures
- **Accessibility**: Ensuring our communication and work is accessible to all
- **Professional Conduct**: Maintaining professional language and tone
- **Acknowledgment**: Crediting and appreciating others' work and ideas
- **Good Faith**: Assuming positive intent in discussions and interactions

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment**: Unwelcome comments, jokes, or actions related to protected characteristics
- **Discrimination**: Exclusion or differential treatment based on identity or characteristics
- **Intimidation**: Threatening language, aggressive behavior, or bullying
- **Sexual Harassment**: Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, or other verbal/physical harassment of a sexual nature
- **Abuse**: Personal attacks, insults, or contemptuous language
- **Exclusion**: Deliberately excluding or margininalizing individuals from discussions or activities
- **Trolling**: Deliberately inflammatory comments intended to provoke conflict
- **Spam**: Repetitive, off-topic, or promotional content that disrupts discussion
- **Doxxing**: Sharing private information without consent
- **Bad Faith Criticism**: Persistent attacks without constructive intent

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All spaces managed by Flood Sentinel (GitHub repository, discussions, issues, pull requests, email communications)
- Any events, workshops, or meetings organized by the project
- Any official project representations on social media or other platforms
- Interactions between community members when representing the project

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it by:

1. **Direct Contact**: Email `erhardik@github.com` with details including:
   - Description of the incident
   - People involved
   - When and where it occurred
   - Any relevant screenshots or evidence (if safe to share)
   - Your contact information

2. **Anonymous Reports**: If you prefer anonymity, you may submit a report through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting or contact a trusted community leader who can relay your report confidentially.

3. **Immediate Safety**: If you feel immediately unsafe, please contact local authorities or emergency services.

### Investigation and Response

Upon receiving a report, the maintainers will:

1. **Acknowledge** receipt within 72 hours
2. **Investigate** the incident fairly and impartially
3. **Communicate** with involved parties (while respecting confidentiality)
4. **Document** findings and actions taken
5. **Enforce** appropriate consequences (see below)
6. **Follow up** to ensure the issue is resolved

### Enforcement Actions

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

1. **Warning**: Private communication explaining the violation and expected behavior
2. **Temporary Restrictions**: Temporary suspension from participation (hours to weeks)
3. **Permanent Ban**: Removal from the project and its communities
4. **Public Communication**: In severe cases, public announcement of enforcement action

The severity of enforcement will depend on:
- Nature and severity of the violation
- Pattern of behavior (first offense vs. repeated)
- Intent and willingness to correct behavior
- Impact on the community

## Restorative Practices

We believe in redemption and growth. When appropriate, we may:

- Offer opportunities for the person to understand the impact of their actions
- Create plans for behavioral change
- Involve mediators to facilitate resolution
- Reintegrate individuals who have shown genuine change

However, some violations (sexual harassment, targeted harassment, threats) may warrant immediate and permanent removal without opportunity for restoration.

## Confidentiality and Privacy

- Reports will be treated confidentially
- Investigation details will be shared only with those who need to know
- Identities of reporters will be protected unless they consent otherwise
- All parties involved deserve respect and privacy

## Innocent Until Proven Otherwise

We acknowledge that:
- False accusations can harm innocent people
- Misunderstandings happen in online communication
- Due process is essential
- We will investigate fairly before taking action

## Acknowledgments

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and inspired by codes of conduct from major open-source projects.

## Changes to This Code

We may update this Code of Conduct as the community evolves. Major changes will be:
- Announced to the community
- Open for feedback
- Effective after a reasonable transition period
